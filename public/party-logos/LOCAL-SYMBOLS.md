# Fruit Heights caucus illustrations

`pinecone.svg` and `sagebrush.svg` reproduce the illustrations published in Fruit
Heights City's May 2023 election guide. They replace the site's original drawings.
The city guide supplies the artwork; it does not identify an artist or state a
separate artwork license. These are credited as city-published illustrations,
without claiming that they are formally adopted party logos.

Source: https://www.fruitheights.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2723/caucus2023
Election information: https://www.fruitheights.gov/313/Elections

The PDF embeds transparent raster illustrations (page 1, image objects 1843 and
1845). Their original images and alpha masks were extracted with PyMuPDF, then
converted to actual SVG paths with VTracer 0.6.15. The SVG files do not embed
bitmap images. Tracing uses color mode, stacked spline paths, speckle filter 2,
color precision 5, layer difference 24, corner threshold 60, length threshold 3.5,
splice threshold 45, and path precision 3.
