The first tranche added 2007–2012 ballot rosters, direct official source links, an importer supporting legacy Excel files, regression checks, and election-year pages through 1994. Municipal coverage is limited to Salt Lake County. Each year below links to its ELUT page and the supporting artifacts. Existing newspaper records are counted separately from these new imports.

Method & validation

Records represent ballot participation, not complete filing lists or inferred winners. General appearances supersede matching primary appearances by normalized name, office, year and party.

First-tranche validation: 13 tests passed, Svelte check reported zero errors and warnings, cached regeneration was byte-for-byte identical, and the production build prerendered 12,119 routes. All 75 House districts were represented in each imported statewide general election.

Published surnames, abbreviations and truncated names are retained. Unresolved ticket identities are not guessed; justice-court sheets were excluded. New source formats require separate validation.

Follow-up phase

The follow-up adds reviewed primary rosters for 1994–2006, the 2000 and 2008 presidential primaries, the 2008 regular primary, selected older general-election rosters, and Salt Lake County municipal rosters for 1997–2005. Each imported PDF transcription links to its supporting page. The table below lists the source PDFs and unresolved portions.

The 1995 year already has 1,225 unofficial newspaper records in the separate dataset: contemporary Deseret News returns. The initial inventory missed this existing coverage; the year page and current inventory now identify it correctly.

The follow-up also removed two Yes/No responses and six recreation-board candidates incorrectly assigned to a preceding council contest in the initial pass. A further five recreation-board entries found during follow-up were also excluded after source review. See the 2007 official general results and 2009 official general results. The initial counts above are preserved as the first-pass snapshot before these corrections.

Follow-up validation: 15 tests passed; Svelte check reported 0 errors and 0 warnings; cached regeneration was byte-for-byte identical; production build and person-merge checks passed, with 12,907 routes prerendered. Validation record.
