Justice Party

The U.S. party was organized in November 2011 with former Salt Lake City mayor Ross C. “Rocky” Anderson among its founders, and nominated Anderson and Luis J. Rodriguez in 2012. Party history. Utah’s imported 2012 records include the Anderson presidential ticket, Daniel Geery for U.S. Senate, and Ty Markham for State House District 73. Official 2012 Utah canvass. Its Wikidata item Q6317052 links to the same U.S. party article. The existing /parties/justice/ route and three candidacy IDs are unchanged.
