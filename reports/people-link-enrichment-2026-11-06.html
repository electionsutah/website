All 4,758 people are inventoried below. This batch prioritizes individually matched government profiles and 2026 candidate websites endorsed by official party directories. Social links were also checked on 13 campaign homepages. Unresearched records are not labeled as having no accounts.

Verification and identity

Links from government and party directories carry the exact endorsing source. Most directory-linked endpoints were not individually fetched; they are directory-endorsed, not tested for live content or activity. Separately inspected campaign pages are identified in each person’s notes. Shared party, chamber, and school-board accounts are not assigned to individuals.

Identity rules and notable exceptions
  • Unique normalized full-name matches are accepted. A missing middle name or initial is accepted only when the given name, surname, and exact 2026 race identify one unique archive record. Nicknames and ambiguous matches are withheld.
  • The archive has separate “Michael Stoddard” (2022) and “Michael R. Stoddard” (2026) records. The current campaign link goes to the unique 2026 record. No people were merged.
  • Libby Shelton’s Facebook control points to a share endpoint, so it was withheld. Her Instagram, Bluesky, TikTok, and X accounts were added.
  • Emily Buss’s embedded YouTube video is hosted by Doug Larson. That channel was excluded. Her own Facebook and Instagram links were added.
  • Taylor Wright’s campaign explicitly links a podcast Instagram and a personal-project YouTube account. They are retained with project labels rather than labeled generic campaign accounts.
  • Kent Udell’s site calls an Instagram button “Facebook”; the URL host correctly determines its Instagram icon. First-party personal Facebook accounts are included only when explicitly endorsed by the campaign.
  • Share/intent endpoints, individual posts and videos, tracking query parameters, duplicate channel aliases, and parent-institution accounts are excluded. Threads and Substack icons were added so those verified accounts can display.
  • Archived filing contacts, biographies, existing editorial overrides, candidate statuses, and identity grouping are unchanged. New links live separately in src/data/person-link-enrichment.json and appear in contact cards, person tiles, and MCP records.
  • No personal phone numbers, home addresses, or email addresses were researched or added. Account access, activity, and future availability were not tested.
