Verification and exceptions
  • All seven websites and the Peoples’ Freedom Instagram account are endorsed by the state’s official party directory. Other socials are linked from the party’s own homepage or contact page; icon-only links were checked in extracted link lists.
  • Forward’s Facebook page and community group are distinct; its alternate embedded YouTube channel URL is omitted. The Green Party’s numeric Facebook embed is omitted as a duplicate. Candidate pages and speech videos on the Libertarian site are excluded.
  • Independent American: homepage and contact checked; no official party social account verified. This does not prove none exists.
  • United Utah: historical party; former domain could not be fetched. Forward’s disclosures document the merger. Successor and legacy accounts are not presented as current United Utah links.
  • Pinecone and Sagebrush: no dedicated accounts verified. City election information remains in their history sections rather than being mislabeled a party website. Nonpartisan, Unaffiliated, and Not reported are categories, not organizations.
  • Instagram cannot be directly inspected by the research service. Peoples’ Freedom is verified through the state’s endorsement, not a live account activity check. Other social destinations are first-party endorsements, not login or activity tests.
  • Added TikTok, Truth Social, and Rumble icon support and included the three previously withheld, White-House-endorsed office links in the office report. That approved batch now has 158 links, including 19 socials.
