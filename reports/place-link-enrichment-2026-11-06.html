Source rules and notable exclusions
  • Only general-government accounts explicitly linked by a government homepage, communication directory, or government-owned social redirect are added. Social accounts were not guessed by handle or copied from Wikipedia.
  • Cache County’s outreach page separates county accounts from sheriff and trails accounts. Only the county accounts are added.
  • Davis’s social directory contains many department and event accounts. County-government LinkedIn and the explicitly labeled County Commission X account are included; health, sheriff, library, tourism, and event accounts are excluded.
  • Beaver’s Instagram shortcut resolves to ramblersutah, a tourism brand. It is withheld. Tooele’s tourism Instagram and manager-specific Facebook are also withheld from the general county collection.
  • Box Elder, Summit, Uintah, Wasatch, and South Jordan publish social shortcuts on their own domains. Redirect destination headers were checked so these resolve to actual platform accounts rather than being mislabeled as websites. Grand’s YouTube redirect was confirmed; its Facebook destination was not confirmed.
  • Sevier’s generic Facebook and Instagram homepage links are not account profiles. San Juan’s social shortcuts did not establish account destinations. These links remain withheld.
  • City Facebook widgets, individual posts, reels, live videos, share buttons, tourism accounts, contractor links, and unverified channel aliases are excluded. Commission and public-meeting accounts are explicitly scoped where needed.
  • Salt Lake County’s homepage and eConnect page did not establish a county-wide social destination. No mayor-named or department account was substituted.
  • Website redirects are used in the link collection; underlying place-profile data is preserved. Existing Wikipedia and Wikidata references remain alongside website/social links. No city inherits its parent county’s social accounts.
  • Source endorsement is verified. Social login, posting activity, and future availability were not tested. Four county sites and Millcreek could not be retrieved through the research service; their existing links remain intact.
