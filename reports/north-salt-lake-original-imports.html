North Salt Lake import investigation

Investigated 2026-11-10. These 30 imported rows incorrectly assigned North Salt Lake to Salt Lake County. Corrected records resolve to Davis County; imported fields and record IDs are preserved. No evidence suggests a historical county change.

Findings

29 newspaper candidate rows (1989–1995): the county assignment came from our import process. The articles list North Salt Lake as a municipality; their North Salt Lake sections do not assign it to Salt Lake County. Checked articles: 1989 returns, 1991 returns, 1993 returns, 1995 returns. The 1991 article explicitly separates Salt Lake County races from the other races, which include North Salt Lake.

Newspaper importer builds a name-keyed lookup from .firecrawl/place-map.json. Duplicate normalized city names silently overwrite earlier entries. Contest county and placeId come from that lookup, then become the candidate county. The committed newspaper extraction manifest already contains salt-lake/north-salt-lake in six contests. This establishes a local assignment, not a county statement copied from the newspaper. The original cached place map and article downloads are absent, so the exact overwrite order cannot be replayed. The pre-existing duplicate is the likely cause, rather than a proven reconstruction of that missing cache.

One 2018 Chris Stewart row: mismatch predates the current correction; ultimate origin remains unresolved. The earliest available repository snapshot, commit 9043dbf, already has city North Salt Lake and county Salt Lake. The 2017–2019 normalizer copies the input county, correcting only the unrelated spelling “Washingotn.” It does not infer Salt Lake County from the city. The legacy data index links the original 2018 export, which now returns 404; the original downloaded JSON is absent. We cannot yet say whether the legacy export or an earlier preparation step introduced the error. A later-linked declaration PDF was not the raw row’s source: its raw source field is empty.

Retrieval limitation: Firecrawl was authenticated with the configured API key but reported zero remaining credits. Public web retrieval supplied all four newspaper articles. No model request costs were measured; no successful Firecrawl scrape was returned.
