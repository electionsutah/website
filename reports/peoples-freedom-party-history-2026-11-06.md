# Peoples’ Freedom Party of Utah: electoral-history review

Reviewed November 6, 2026.

ELUT entities: [Peoples’ Freedom Party](https://www.electionsutah.org/parties/peoples-freedom-party/), [Claudia De La Cruz](https://www.electionsutah.org/people/claudia-de-la-cruz/), and [Karina Alexandra Garcia](https://www.electionsutah.org/people/karina-alexandra-garcia/).

## Finding

No documented candidate running under Utah’s Peoples’ Freedom Party name was found. The evidence supports “no candidacies identified in the records reviewed,” rather than the absolute assertion that the party has never run, endorsed, or supported any candidate. Our existing current, historical, local, and researched candidate datasets have no matching party records. The latest official 2026 filing page and October workbook also have no matching party rows. The general-election certification names the party only in its registered-party directory. [Official filing list](https://vote.utah.gov/2026-candidate-filings/); [October workbook](https://vote.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Candidate-Filing-2026.xlsx); [ballot certification](https://vote.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-General-Election-Certification.pdf).

## Documented history and people

- November 11, 2025: KRCL interviewed Adrian Rollins, an Air Force veteran and PSL Salt Lake organizer, describing him as helping start the People’s Freedom Party. Its event listing advertised a November 13 signature drive at the Salt Lake Liberation Center, 352 Denver Street. Organizers sought 2,000 registered-voter signatures by November 30. This establishes organizing activity by November 2025, not an exact legal founding date. It does not establish that Rollins was president or sole founder. [KRCL founding coverage](https://www.krcl.org/blog/defining-military-victory-to-starting-about-face-slc/).
- January 29, 2026: Independent Political Report described the organization as launched the previous year and seeking ballot access; it listed it among supporters of the January 30 national shutdown. [Independent Political Report](https://independentpoliticalreport.com/2026/01/multiple-political-parties-and-caucuses-endorse-january-30-national-shutdown/).
- January 30, 2026: The Salt Lake Tribune independently reported Peoples’ Freedom Party Utah among supporters of the national shutdown. This establishes public organizing activity, not a candidate nomination. [Salt Lake Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/politics/2026/01/30/national-shutdown-utah-protesters/).
- February 4, 2026: City Cast Salt Lake published “Meet Utah’s Newest Political Party,” interviewing spokesperson Denise Weaver. The episode description identifies the party and her role. The audio was not transcribed or reviewed in this investigation; detailed platform or campaign claims cannot be inferred from its description. [City Cast interview](https://saltlake.citycast.fm/podcasts/meet-utahs-newest-political-party).
- April 10, 2026: KRCL identified Denise Weaver as president of the People’s Freedom Party and as a PSL Salt Lake organizer. This is a dated leadership identification, not independent verification of her continued tenure in November. [KRCL leadership coverage](https://www.krcl.org/blog/of-kids-and-kids-with-roots-charter-high/).
- August 28, 2026 certification, published in September: Utah’s general-election certification lists Peoples’ Freedom Party / PFP as a registered party. No candidate in that document is identified with it. [Official ballot certification](https://vote.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-General-Election-Certification.pdf).
- Current state party directory: recognizes the party and links to its Instagram account. Exact qualification date, petition acceptance date, constitution, complete officers list, and convention records remain unverified. [State party directory](https://vote.utah.gov/more-information-on-utah-political-parties/).

The KRCL sources demonstrate overlapping organizers and a PSL-hosted petition event. They do not by themselves establish a formal national affiliation, merger, or legal identity between PFP and PSL.

## Earlier electoral connection

Utah’s official 2024 Certificate of Ascertainment lists a DENISE WEAVER among presidential-elector nominees for Claudia De la Cruz and Karina Alexandra Garcia. The ticket received 3,189 Utah votes and is grouped with candidates not affiliated with a registered political party. The name matches the later PFP president, but this document alone does not independently establish personal identity. Even if confirmed, it would demonstrate an individual’s earlier electoral involvement, not a 2024 PFP candidacy. [Official 2024 Certificate of Ascertainment](https://www.archives.gov/files/electoral-college/2024/ascertainment-utah.pdf).

## Coverage and limits

Checked party-name and apostrophe variants, PFP, named organizers, founding and leadership references, candidate/convention announcements, the state directory, the latest 2026 filing page and workbook, the certification, and earlier imported records. The previous source audit scanned 224 cached county feeds: Weber’s general-election party lookup includes PFP, but no other object references that party ID. Lookup-table presence is not a candidate record.

Instagram direct retrieval returned HTTP 429. Public search did not surface a verified candidate announcement. State November 2 registration statistics report zero active and inactive PFP registrants ([state registration statistics](https://vote.utah.gov/current-voter-registration-statistics/)); this does not prove the organization has no supporters or has never endorsed a candidate. Do not infer a founding date from search-engine publication dates on reused forms. Do not conflate this Utah party with California’s Peace and Freedom Party, the FEC’s American People’s Freedom Party, or foreign parties sharing the name.

No candidate records or public-facing party profile were changed. Definitive follow-up evidence would be the lieutenant governor’s party-registration/qualification file and officer submissions, or a party-issued nominee/endorsement list. No outreach was sent.
