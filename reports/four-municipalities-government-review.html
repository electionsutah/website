Four municipalities: government-source review

Reviewed November 6, 2026. 17 missing candidate records across eight municipal contests are confirmed in government-hosted results. Alton remains unresolved. Imported all 17 verified historical candidate records. All eight municipal result tables are now imported and shown on place pages, with links to all 17 candidates.

Elmo

Confirmed missing 2017 mayor and council contests: five named candidates. The county document filename and November 7 date identify the general election, but its internal heading incorrectly says Primary. Treat this metadata discrepancy explicitly during import. The four council rows include write-ins Bert Reed and Ella Ekker; a tie at 23 means winner status must not be inferred without seat count and any tie resolution. The 2019 county results include an Elmo precinct for a district bond vote, not an Elmo municipal candidate contest. 2023 county results cover Emery and Ferron only; absence does not prove Elmo had no candidates.

Clawson

Confirmed missing 2019 mayoral two-year, council two-year, and council four-year contests: five named candidates. Gary Price and Jerry Price are explicitly write-ins. Totals include county canvass adjustments: Gary Price 42+2=44; Bill Wagner 23+2=25. The 2025 cancellation minutes confirm cancellation but still provide no named roster.

Lynndyl

Confirmed missing 2021 mayoral, council two-year, and council four-year contests: seven named candidates. Official municipal results are distinct from county precinct reports. The 2015-uploaded Lynndyl-OR.pdf is actually November 4, 2014 county/state/federal results and must not be imported as a 2015 municipal election. Catalog entries for 2017 cancellation, 2019 cancellation and Amber Lloyd declaration currently return 404; these are leads, not verified source records. The 2025 signed cancellation resolution still names no candidates, and the January 2026 mayoral process is appointment, not a public election.

Alton

Still unresolved for named municipal candidates in the current archive period. Kane County’s 2025 certified summary and sample ballot have no Alton municipal contest. The county Local Information link labeled Town of Alton resolves to tourism information, not a town election archive. The state Public Notice body index contains contact details but no linked meeting notices in its sitemap view. Utah State Archives identifies public council minutes (series 24273) and ordinances/resolutions (series 24185), including microfilm holdings; finding aids establish that records exist but do not themselves supply named candidates. Absence of online returns cannot establish no election or no candidates.

Review limitations

  • Government archive indexes are incomplete; no verified records is not equivalent to no election.
  • Emery County current results index omits a 2021 municipal file and offers no 2013/2015 municipal returns.
  • Lynndyl 2017/2019 county attachment catalog entries and image derivatives return 404.
  • Alton archive inventories describe public microfilm holdings, not a verified online candidate roster.
  • Municipal results now use a separate sourced dataset and place-page tables; they are not mixed into statewide comparison baselines.
