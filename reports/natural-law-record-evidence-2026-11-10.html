Vote tables are present in those canvasses, but the corresponding general elections are not in the site’s structured results index. See the source beside each record below.

William Scott Shields: corrected identity display and link

The 1996 official canvass abbreviates the governor/lieutenant-governor ticket as “Lesh & Shields” under “NL” and records 2,969 votes for that joint ticket. Official result, page 5. Contemporary reporting names Robert C. Lesh and William Scott Shields as the governor and lieutenant-governor candidates. Deseret News, June 22, 1996.

The existing William Scott Shields record now displays his full name. The party description links that full phrase, and the ticket’s running-mate display uses the same reviewed identity. The existing /people/shields/ route and filing ID are preserved. The imported source remains “Shields.” Other Shields candidates are not merged.

What remains unavailable in the site

There are no structured 1996, 1998, or 2000 general-election snapshots in the results index. The separate 2000 primary snapshot does not cover these general-election records. Consequently these candidates’ pages cannot yet show imported result tables for those appearances. The PDF sources remain accessible from each candidate/ticket record.

The current ten entries are selected reviewed roster coverage, not a complete claim about every Natural Law candidate ever filed in Utah. No separate candidacy declarations were found in the existing document mappings. Other abbreviated source identities, including “Lesh,” “Hagelin,” “Tompkins,” and “Goldhaber,” still need individual review; this change does not silently merge them.
