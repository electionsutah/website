Elections Utah · Working-tree audit · November 10, 2026

The party update works. Contrast and narrow-screen layout need attention.

The Natural Law Party name, logo, and consolidated sources are present in the running site. The highest-priority finding is insufficient contrast for links on its tinted hero. Six additional findings concern reflow, search layout, content consistency, and mobile task placement.

Read the findings · Validation evidence · Download browser measurements (JSON)

Scope: Local site at localhost:5173, including the uncommitted changes from this thread. Eight representative routes at 320, 768, and 1440 CSS pixels, plus Labs at 320/1440 and glossary and historical-person spot checks. Browser: Synaptic Code preview, Chromium 152. The target is WCAG 2.2 AA product quality. This is a sampled expert review, not a complete conformance assessment or participant usability study. No application code was changed during the audit.

What is working

  • Natural Law Party retains its route, displays the 156 × 156 supplied logo without distortion, and presents one source trigger instead of three repeated links. The logo also appears in the party directory and Catherine Carter’s candidacy.
  • The evidence popover names its three sources, opens beside its trigger, admits keyboard focus with Tab, and closes with Escape. Focus returns to the source trigger. Its mobile content fits its own width; the surrounding page has the reflow issue below.
  • Mobile navigation opens with an exposed expanded state and closes with Escape. Search focuses its labeled input, makes the header/main/footer inert, exposes a named modal, finds Natural Law Party, and restores focus on Escape.
  • Party list view exposes its selected state. The 1996 filter updates the URL and shows 6 of 11 people. The search empty state announces 0 of 11 and the clear-search control recovers the list. The global empty state gives useful search suggestions.
  • Sampled pages have one main landmark, one H1, descriptive titles, labeled controls, and no detected nested links/buttons. The glossary and global search fields have visible 3px parent focus outlines.
  • At 768 and 1440 pixels, the eight sampled routes have no document-width overflow. Person photos initially flagged by the simple image probe were lazy-loaded; scrolling loaded the sampled photos successfully.

Prioritized findings

Priorities reflect task impact: P1 should be addressed before release; P2 is the next correction pass; P3 is a usability refinement. Observations are from the browser unless explicitly marked as expert judgment.

7 findings
P1 · Accessibility · Observed

1. Hero links fall below minimum text contrast

Where: Natural Law Party, including the 12px sources button and 16px linked names/years. Computed foreground is #006eb7; computed background is color(srgb 0.891373 0.887451 0.871373). Their calculated contrast is 4.15:1, below 4.5:1 for this text. This is a measured SC 1.4.3 failure in the sampled state; see W3C’s contrast criterion.

Reproduce: Open the party profile and inspect the hero’s computed colors; calculate relative luminance using the sRGB transfer function. Recommended correction: scope --color-blue-dark (#015086) to text links and the source trigger on tinted heroes. It yields about 6.52:1 on this background. Recheck all party tints and interaction states. Preserve the existing palette. Evidence: hero/color styles and party source trigger.

P2 · Reflow · Observed

2. The body’s minimum width creates horizontal scrolling at 320 pixels

Where: all eight core route samples at the narrow width. innerWidth=320, documentElement.clientWidth=305, and body/document scroll width 320. A horizontal scrollbar is visible in the mobile screenshot. The global body{min-width:320px} exceeds the available layout width when the vertical scrollbar occupies 15px.

Impact: unnecessary two-dimensional scrolling at narrow sizes or high zoom. Recommended correction: allow the body to shrink to available width and verify child controls at that width; do not conceal overflow globally. In anchoredPopover, use the available layout viewport consistently when clamping overlays. Test both classic and overlay scrollbars. Standard: SC 1.4.10 Reflow. The observed failure is specific to this browser/scrollbar configuration.

P2 · Search layout · Observed

3. Mobile search shortcut cards exceed their grid

Reproduce: at 320px, open global search with no query. Its panel is 305px wide and its shortcut grid is 265px wide (x=20…285), but the right-hand shortcut links extend to x=320. The panel’s scroll width is 320px. The People count and label crowd the right edge.

Impact: the entry point for browsing records is cramped and introduces horizontal scrolling within the modal. Recommended correction: use repeat(2,minmax(0,1fr)), allow the count/label arrangement to wrap, and use one column when content requires it. Inspect .search-start grid/card rules. Retain the existing named search dialog and focus behavior.

P2 · Archive scope · Observed

4. Archive-range copy no longer describes the available records

Home and search describe an “1989–2026” archive, while U.S. House District 2 lists records as early as 1960. Readers may stop searching for earlier records because the navigation copy suggests they are outside the archive.

Recommended correction: derive the displayed year span from the available data while retaining “selected cycles” and incomplete-coverage language. Use the same scope text in home, search, directories, documentation, and metadata. Evidence: home copy, metadata descriptions, and the office’s visible filing years. Heuristic: match the system’s description to what users can actually find.

P2 · Classification · Observed

5. A federal office is labeled both federal and state

The U.S. House District 2 hero reads “Federal office · State office.” The same profile correctly describes a federal congressional seat. Imported source groupings are leaking into the office’s user-facing classification.

Recommended correction: derive jurisdiction from the normalized office identity and preserve original source sections in evidence. Apply the same classification to directory filters. Review the office aggregation and office eyebrow. The current boundary-versus-historical-filing caveats are useful and should remain.

P2 · Identity consistency · Observed

6. Full historical names lead to surname-only records

The party description says “William Scott Shields,” but only “Shields” is linked, opening a Shields record. That record has the expected 1996 Natural Law lieutenant-governor ticket, so this check did not establish a wrong-person link. Its running mate is “Lesh,” whereas the description links a separate Robert C. Lesh record.

Impact: the transition weakens identity recognition and may fragment a person’s history. Recommended correction: review the full names against the contemporary Utah report and the 1996 canvass; apply reviewed display names/aliases and link the full name, preserving raw ticket labels and established routes. Do not merge people through surname matching. Implementation evidence: automatic prose linking and historical ticket data.

P3 · Task placement · Expert judgment

7. The mobile party introduction delays the candidate list

At 320px, the Natural Law hero extends to roughly y=941, and candidate search starts around y=1350. The logo, long historical paragraph, source control, statistics, and section spacing all precede the primary browsing task. The heading and logo remain readable; this is a task-order refinement, not evidence of a failed user session.

Recommended correction: add a compact “Browse candidates” anchor near the heading or shorten the introduction and move detailed context into the existing evidence popover. Retain accessible evidence and the explanation of people versus filings. Validate the preferred option with a short task study before changing the broader visual system.

Suggested usability check

Ask voters, researchers, and a keyboard/screen-reader user to: find Catherine Carter’s 1996 party; confirm the source of the party description; filter party candidates to 1996; find an earlier-than-1989 congressional record; and explain why there are 10 filings but 11 people. Observe completion, wrong turns, evidence discovery, and recovery. Test narrow and wide screens. This is a proposed study; no participants were recruited or observed.

Primary guidance verified during this audit: W3C contrast minimum, reflow, and target size minimum. Inline text links have a target-size exception; the audit does not treat every text link shorter than 44px as a WCAG failure. The linked public ELUT routes identify the relevant entities; measurements describe the local working tree, which may differ from production.
